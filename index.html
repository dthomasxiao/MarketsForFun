Week of November 2, 2026 · by Thomas

Equities slid 2.3% as oil and gold pulled in opposite directions

Democrats’ sweep of the midterms set the tone. Stocks first rallied 1.8% on hopes for clearer, less volatile policy, de-escalation abroad, and a possible rollback of tariffs. They then fell 4% as investors worried that lower AI spending could also limit economic output. Compounded, that leaves equities down about 2.3% on the week.

Oil fell, gold rose

WTI dropped 6% from $90.34 as de-escalation efforts in the Middle East and the midterm results eased supply fears. Gold gained 4.1% as falling oil lowered inflation expectations.

What I’m watching

The data points toward a tighter Fed, while oil and gold are pricing in softer inflation. If those two signals keep disagreeing, next week’s inflation data decides which one the market follows.
